Terms & Privacy Policy

CodeByTushu • Gym Plan Platform

1. Athletic Use & Service Scope

Gym Plan is designed as an offline-first athletic workout tracker, reverse pyramid training log, and nutrition planner. By using Gym Plan, you agree to follow the training guidelines responsibly.

2. Health & Exercise Disclaimer

Exercises, set calculations, and dietary suggestions are provided for general conditioning and fitness reference. Users are encouraged to perform adequate warmups and consult a healthcare professional before undertaking rigorous weight training.

3. Data Ownership & Backups

Your workout history belongs exclusively to you. You are free to export all logs at any time as JSON or synchronize your account to Google Firebase Cloud for permanent multi-device access.

1. Local-First Privacy

Gym Plan stores your workout logs, weight milestones, calisthenics records, and dietary preferences directly on your device. We do not sell, rent, or track your personal fitness logs for advertising.

2. Google Cloud Sync

When you opt to sign in with your Google account, your profile and workout database are encrypted and backed up to Google Firebase Firestore under your private account ID, ensuring you never lose your progress.

3. User Right to Erasure

You can perform a complete hard reset or delete your local and cloud records at any time directly through the Personal Dashboard.
