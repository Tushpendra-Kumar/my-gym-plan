🏆 Virat Kohli Athletic Guide

🔥 7 - 12 - 15 Reps Science (Reverse Pyramid)

Aapka 7, 12, 15 ka plan scientifically bohot zabardast hai:

  • Set 1 (Heavy 7 Reps @ 80%): Raw strength aur fast-twitch muscle fibers trigger karta hai jisse athletic power aati hai.
  • Set 2 (Moderate 12 Reps @ 70%): Muscle hypertrophy (shape aur size) build karta hai.
  • Set 3 (Burnout 15 Reps @ 60%): Muscle endurance, vascularity aur deep burn deta hai bina joint pain ke.
  • Note: Set 1 se pehle hamesha 2 light warm-up sets karein taaki injury ka koi chance na rahe.

⚡ 6-Day Athletic Schedule Ka Logic

  • Mon (Chest): Push power & core stability.
  • Tue (Back): V-Taper posture & pulling strength (deadlifts).
  • Wed (Shoulders + Core): 3D delts & rotational bat/throw power.
  • Thu (Arms & Grip): Biceps, triceps & crushing forearm grip.
  • Fri (Functional HIIT): Kettlebells, box jumps, sprints & fat burning.
  • Sat (Legs): Squats & single-leg balance for speed & jumping.
  • Sun (Recovery): Foam rolling & mobility flow.

🥗 Virat Kohli Nutrition & Recovery Rules

  • Protein: 1.6g to 2g per kg bodyweight (Eggs, Paneer/Tofu, Soya, Chicken/Fish, Whey).
  • Hydration: Workout ke dauran 1-1.5L paani aur din bhar me 3.5-4L paani zaroori hai.
  • Rest: 7-8 ghante ki uninterrupted neend. Muscles gym me nahi, sote waqt recover aur grow hote hain.
